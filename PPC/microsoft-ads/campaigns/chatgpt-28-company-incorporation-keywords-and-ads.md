## Compliance flags
- "Lowest Pricing Guaranteed", "Lowest Fees\!" and "Affordable Pricing" as a comparative claim: Consumer Protection Act, 2019 s.2(28); CCPA Misleading Ads Guidelines 2022; ASCI Code Ch. I (substantiation) and Ch. IV (comparisons; verify). Banned by `PPC/CLAUDE.md` unless substantiated.
- "Trusted by 10,000+ Startups\!": unsubstantiated, and inconsistent with the site's "18K+ clients" claim (itself TODO evidence).
- "Get Your Pvt Ltd Registered in 7 Days.": the timeline depends on MCA. Same provisions as above.
