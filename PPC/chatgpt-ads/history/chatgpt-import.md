### DPDP flag on #1 (CRM leads → ChatGPT Ads audience)
Uploading Zoho CRM lead emails and phone numbers to OpenAI for ad matching is processing of digital personal data under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**:
- **s.4 (lawful purpose):** processing is allowed only for a lawful purpose, with consent (s.6) or for a "legitimate use" (s.7). Ad targeting is not one of the s.7 legitimate uses, so consent is the likely route.
- **s.5 (notice):** the notice must state the personal data and the purpose. The website lead form's privacy note reads "Details used only to respond to your enquiry." (`shared/business-context.md`). That covers responding to the enquiry, not advertising or audience matching. Leads collected under that notice should **not** be uploaded for targeting without a fresh notice and consent.
- **s.6 (consent):** consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, by clear affirmative action, and limited to the specified purpose. Withdrawal must be as easy as giving it (s.6(4)). This includes Lost leads and opt-outs (Sanjeev excluded Lost; ChatGPT also said to exclude opt-outs).
- **s.8 (Data Fiduciary obligations):** Setindiabiz stays responsible when it shares data with a Data Processor (s.8(1)), and processing must run under a valid contract (s.8(2)). Data must be accurate where used to make decisions (s.8(3)), protected by reasonable security safeguards (s.8(5)), and erased once the purpose is served or consent is withdrawn (s.8(7)). OpenAI's terms for audience matching need review on this basis.
- **s.16 (cross-border transfer):** check whether any restriction has been notified for the destination country.
- **DPDP Rules, 2025** (notified Nov 2025) commence in phases:
  - the definitional and Board provisions immediately
  - Consent Manager provisions after about 12 months (around Nov 2026)
  - most Data Fiduciary obligations, including Rule 3 notice content, security and retention, after about 18 months (around May 2027)
- **Verify the current version and commencement status on meity.gov.in before acting.** Even before full commencement, build the notice and consent now, because the leads in question were collected under an enquiry-only notice.
- **Repo rule:** never commit the list or any identifiers. ChatGPT itself said to upload directly in Ads Manager and not to share the file in chat.

## Compliance flags (ad copy in these chats)
- **Implied legal practice:** #15 copy uses "India-Qualified Attorneys", "attorney-led", "India-qualified legal & compliance team", "Talk to India Counsel" and "India law advice; not US legal advice". Under the **Advocates Act, 1961 s.29 and s.33**, only enrolled advocates may practise law. **Bar Council of India Rules, Part VI Ch. II, Rule 36** bars advocates from soliciting or advertising. Do not run these claims unless they are accurate and permitted (verify the current BCI position). They also raise the OpenAI legal-services policy risk (#30).
- **Unsubstantiated claims:** "Fixed-Fee" / "Fixed fees" (#15) conflicts with the site's "free written estimate, no published package prices". "in 10–15 days" (#15) is a timeline claim. Both need substantiation under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019 s.2(28)** (misleading advertisement; CCPA powers under s.21), the **CCPA Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements, 2022** (conditions for a non-misleading ad; substantiation) and the **ASCI Code, Chapter I** (truthful and honest representation; substantiation).
- **Government affiliation:** #30's table correctly says to avoid "Government-approved agent". Keep that rule. Ads for ROC/GST/MCA filings should not imply government affiliation (ASCI Code Ch. I; CCPA 2022 Guidelines).
- **Cloaking:** #30 warns "Do not disguise a service page as an education page". Educational pages must be genuinely educational.
