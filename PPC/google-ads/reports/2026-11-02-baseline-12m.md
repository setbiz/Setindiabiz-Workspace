## Lessons for the next Google launch (to be pressure-tested by the Goldfish)
1. Trademark: cheap clicks (₹16.5 CPC, 11.8% CTR) but ₹1,893 per platform conversion and no CRM sales. Suspect a query-intent mismatch (DIY / "trademark search" / "free" traffic) or lead handling. Pull search terms before relaunch.
2. Subsidiary: ₹375 CPC with 75 clicks. Low-volume, high-competition keywords. Needs a tighter keyword set, a dedicated landing page and a capped test budget.
3. Before any relaunch: enhanced conversions + offline conversion import from Zoho, so bidding optimises for qualified leads and won deals, not form fills.
