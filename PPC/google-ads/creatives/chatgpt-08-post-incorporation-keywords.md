## Compliance notes
- "INC-20A Filing Online" and "Appoint first auditor" are filing-type keywords. They are the kind of query most likely to hit Google's Government documents and official services policy. Pair them with advisory copy ("Post-incorporation compliance support"), never "file with MCA".
- **Customer Match from the MCA director list:** DPDP Act 2023 ss.4–6 and s.8 apply (s.3(c)(ii) needs legal review). Google's Customer Match policy also requires that data was collected with consent for this use. Verify both.

### Google PPC targeting (turn 1)
> Custom Audience Upload: Use director mobile/emails for customer match (YouTube, Gmail, Search).
> Search Ads: Based on keywords like:
> "INC-20A Filing Online"
> "ROC compliance for new company"
> "Appoint first auditor"
> "Trademark registration after incorporation"
> "Monthly GST return filing for Pvt Ltd"
> Location Targeting: Pan-India (filter high incorporations states like Maharashtra, Delhi, Gujarat, TN, UP)

### Display campaign (turn 1)
> Target users by: Recently visited "company registration" sites; Interests in entrepreneurship, startups, finance, compliance
> Placements on: Startup blogs; CA discussion forums; Legal info websites

### YouTube without paid Google (turn 2)
> Instead of paid Google campaigns, go with: Sponsorships or shoutouts on startup/legal/CA YouTube channels; Run video explainers and use links in the description to your services

Influencer or sponsored content needs an ASCI disclosure label: ASCI Guidelines for Influencer Advertising in Digital Media (2021, updated since; verify current version) and the CCPA Endorsement guidelines (2022/2023).
