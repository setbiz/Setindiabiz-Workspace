## Compliance flags
- **Representation claims** ("represent them before GST Authorities", "Attend personal hearing", "Personal Hearing Assistance"): under CGST Act s.116, only an authorised representative (for example, an advocate, CA, CS, CMA or enrolled GST practitioner) may appear. The ad and page must say the work is done by such professionals. Verify the current s.116 text.
- **"Fast Turnaround"**, **"Can GST registration be restored? Yes."**: outcome and speed claims. Avoid implying an assured restoration. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 s.2(28); CCPA 2022 Guidelines; ASCI Code Ch. I.
- **"You have only 7 Working Days"**: accurate only if it matches the notice. CGST Rules, 2017 r.22(1) gives seven working days to reply to REG-17 (verify current text). Urgency copy must be true (CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023, "false urgency").
- **Google "Government documents and official services" policy**: GST registration restoration and revocation are government services. Check the policy applies to "assistance with" ads and whether certification is needed. Never imply GSTN/CBIC affiliation (Emblems and Names Act, 1950 s.3).
