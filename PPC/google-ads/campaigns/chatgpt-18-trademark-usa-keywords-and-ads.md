## Compliance flags
- **Google Ads "Government documents and official services" policy** was the stated constraint (turn 4). Trademark filing with the Indian Trade Marks Registry is an official service. Check the current policy text and whether advertiser verification or certification applies when targeting the USA or India.
- **CTA "Start Your Application"** (turn 4) and callout "100% Compliance" (turn 5). The first can read as if Setindiabiz is the filing authority, and the second is a guarantee claim. Prefer "Get Expert Help" / "Free Consultation". Consumer Protection Act, 2019 s.2(28); CCPA 2022 Guidelines; ASCI Code Ch. I.
- **"Trusted Trademark Services for US Companies"** implies a US client base. Substantiate it (US client testimonials) or drop it.
- **Implied government affiliation:** never use "official", the IP India or Trade Marks Registry name/logo, or the State Emblem. Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950 s.3; State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005 (verify current versions).
