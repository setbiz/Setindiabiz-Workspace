Facebook audience settings and interest terms, turn 4, ChatGPT

Start Broad and Narrow Down:

Instead of trying to pinpoint the exact perfect audience right away, begin with a more general group that covers your industry and location. For example, you could target people in India (or a specific region) who are interested in topics like “business services,” “compliance,” “startups,” or “entrepreneurship.” Once you see how that broad audience performs, start layering in more specific factors—like age ranges, job titles, or specific interests—to refine it.

  

Leverage Lookalike Audiences:

If you have a list of existing clients, email subscribers, or website visitors, you can create a “Lookalike Audience.” This lets Facebook find users who share similar characteristics to your current customers. It’s a great way to reach people who are likely to be interested in your services without guessing too much.

  

Use Custom Audiences and Retargeting:

If you’ve had visitors to your website, set up a retargeting audience. Facebook’s Pixel can track these visitors and show ads only to them. This helps you focus on an audience that already knows about you, making it more likely they’ll convert.

  

Pay Attention to Demographics and Interests:

Consider who typically uses your services. Are they small business owners? Entrepreneurs? What age range are they in? Are there specific industries that frequently need compliance and legal assistance? By jotting down these traits, you can better select Facebook’s demographic and interest categories that match your target audience.

  

Experiment with Split Testing:

Run small, split tests on different audience settings. For example, test one ad set targeting “Entrepreneurship” and another targeting “Business Management” or “Legal Services.” See which one gets better engagement and adjust your strategy accordingly.

LinkedIn cost benchmarks and saving tips, turn 5, ChatGPT

CPC (Cost Per Click):

$5–$10 per click is a common benchmark, though it can go higher in competitive industries or for highly specific targeting.

  

CPM (Cost Per 1,000 Impressions):

$6–$12 is a common range for impressions-based campaigns, though again, this can fluctuate based on your audience size and the relevance of your targeting criteria.

  

Lead Gen Form Costs:

If you’re using LinkedIn’s lead generation forms, the cost per lead typically ranges from $30–$100 depending on the industry, campaign optimization, and the quality of the targeting.

  

Why LinkedIn Costs More:

LinkedIn is more expensive because it’s a professional network. It allows you to target users by their job title, company size, industry, and seniority level, which can yield higher-quality leads. Many advertisers find that even though the CPC is higher, the resulting conversions or leads are more valuable because they’re reaching the exact audience they need.

  

Cost-Saving Tips:

  

Start with a lower daily budget and optimize based on performance.

Test multiple ad formats (Sponsored Content, Message Ads, Text Ads) to find the most cost-effective option.

Refine your targeting over time to focus on the segments that convert best, reducing wasteful clicks.
