Important corrections before publishing

Do not say “₹100/day, no upper cap” as a simple penalty line. Section 35 now provides ₹100/day penalty with statutory caps, while MCA additional filing fees apply separately under the LLP Rules depending on delay and LLP category. Safer ad wording: “Late filing attracts additional MCA fees and statutory penalty.”

India Code

\+1

Use “Total from ₹2,049”, not “Total ₹2,049”, because MCA fee can vary by contribution slab from ₹50 to ₹600.

Lunawat & Co.

In the image showing 30th May 2025, change it to 30 May 2026. Form 11 is due within 60 days from financial year closure, so for FY 2025–26 the due date is 30 May 2026.

India Code

Avoid direct attack lines such as “Tired of CAs…” or “unlike others”. Use comparison without naming CAs or competitors.
