Creative review and changes, turn 1, ChatGPT:

3\. Creative review and changes

Global corrections for all creatives

  

Use “additional fees” instead of “penalties” or “late fee penalties”. Under CCFS, the key concession is on additional filing fees, not necessarily every statutory penalty, adjudicated penalty or prosecution exposure.

  

Add a small footer disclaimer:

“Subject to eligibility under MCA General Circular No. 01/2026. Normal filing fees apply.”

  

Avoid heavy reliance on “Did you miss…” or “Is your company…” because Meta can treat direct second-person language as implying personal or business distress. Safer phrasing:

“Pending ROC filings? CCFS 2026 may help eligible companies.”

  

Avoid using the MCA logo, Government of India emblem or official-looking seals in paid ads unless you have explicit authorisation. The State Emblem of India law prohibits improper or commercial use that may create an impression of government association.

India Code

  

Creative-wise comments

  

Creatives 1 and 2:

These are the cleanest and most ad-friendly. Keep them. Change “late fees” to “additional fees”. Add “Subject to eligibility” in small text.

  

Creative 3:

Too crowded. “Regularise ROC Filing” should be “Regularise ROC Filings”. The bottom has too many benefit blocks. Remove either “Dormant Status Assistance” or “Strike-Off Guidance” if this ad set is only for annual filing leads.

  

Creative 4:

Good for dormant/inactive company ad set. Correct “MSc-1” to “MSC-1” and “Stk-2” to “STK-2”. Replace “Company Not Active?” with “Inactive Company? Review CCFS 2026 Options”.

  

Creative 5:

Strong visual, but text-heavy. Replace “Pay only 10% of additional fees / 50% off / 75% off” with more precise wording:

  

Pending filings: Pay only 10% additional fees

Dormant status: 50% fee concession

Strike-off: 75% fee concession

  

Creative 6:

Good structure. Replace “Late Fees” with “Additional Fees”. The “90%” is prominent and can work well for lead generation.

  

Creative 7:

Good urgency creative. Reduce fear language like “penalties keep increasing” to “additional fees may continue under normal provisions after the scheme window.” It is more professional and safer.

  

Creative 8:

Visually strong but remove the MCA/Government logo block. Replace it with “Based on MCA General Circular No. 01/2026” or “CCFS 2026 Compliance Window”. Also add “not a government website” if the landing page could be confused as official.
