Ministry of Corporate Affairs - Company Incorporation

  

Registering a company in India is a structured process governed by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). Here's a step-by-step guide to assist you:

  

Obtain a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC):

Since the registration process is entirely online, a DSC is required for signing electronic documents. Authorized certifying agencies issue DSCs.

ClearTax

  

Acquire a Director Identification Number (DIN):

DIN is a unique identifier for directors. It can be obtained by filing the SPICe+ (INC-32) form during the company incorporation process.

ClearTax

  

Register on the MCA Portal:

Create an account on the MCA portal to access various e-filing services.

ClearTax

  

Reserve the Company Name:

Use the 'Reserve Unique Name' (RUN) service on the MCA portal to check and reserve your desired company name. Ensure the name adheres to the Companies Act, 2013 naming guidelines.

Startup India

  

Prepare the Incorporation Documents:

  

Memorandum of Association (MoA): Defines the company's objectives and scope.

Articles of Association (AoA): Outlines the company's internal management rules.

These documents can be filed electronically using the SPICe+ forms.

Startup India

  

File the SPICe+ Form:

The SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically) form integrates various services:

  

Company name reservation

Incorporation

DIN allotment

PAN and TAN application

EPFO and ESIC registration

Professional tax registration (for specific states)

Bank account opening

This form streamlines the incorporation process.

ClearTax

  

Obtain the Certificate of Incorporation:

Upon approval, the Registrar of Companies (RoC) issues the Certificate of Incorporation, which includes the company's Corporate Identity Number (CIN). Additionally, PAN and TAN are allotted.

ClearTax

  

Post-Incorporation Compliance:

After incorporation, ensure compliance with:

  

Appointment of an auditor within 30 days.

Filing annual returns and financial statements.

Maintaining statutory registers and records.

  

For detailed information and updates, refer to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs - Company Incorporation.

  

Please note that procedures and requirements may change. Consulting with a legal professional or chartered accountant is advisable to ensure compliance with all regulations.
