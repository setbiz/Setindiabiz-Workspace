Engagement ads vs broadcast explanation, turn 2, ChatGPT

Short answer: No—Engagement ads aren’t a broadcast.

They’re a Meta campaign objective that shows an ad on FB/IG and nudges people to interact (e.g., open a WhatsApp chat, like/comment, watch a video). If someone taps your ad, it opens a 1:1 chat with your business; you are not pushing a message to everyone on a list.

Facebook

\+2

  

Engagement ads vs “broadcast”

Engagement (Click-to-WhatsApp) ad → Ad appears to a targeted audience → user taps → chat starts with you in WhatsApp. Great for starting conversations and capturing opt-in. Not outbound messaging.

WhatsApp Business

Broadcast / outbound messaging → You send one-to-many messages to contacts who’ve already opted in (and, in the WhatsApp Business app’s broadcast lists, they must have your number saved; max 256 per list). For API use, you send pre-approved message templates (marketing/utility) to opted-in users, including outside the 24-hour window.

WhatsApp Help Center

\+2

  

When to use what

  

Use Engagement → Click-to-WhatsApp to attract directors and get them to message you first.

Facebook

Use template messages (API) later to proactively reach those who opted in—that’s the broadcast-like part, but it’s governed by WhatsApp template rules.

Facebook Developers

  

How an engagement ad reaches the audience, turn 3, ChatGPT

It’s something they see on Facebook/Instagram (and sometimes in WhatsApp Status)—not a message you push to them.

  

How it works, step-by-step

  

Your ad appears in placements like FB/IG Feed, Stories/Reels, Marketplace (and Meta also supports ads that appear in WhatsApp Status). The ad has a CTA like “Send WhatsApp Message.”

WhatsApp Business

\+2

When someone taps the CTA, a 1:1 WhatsApp chat opens with your business (often with a pre-filled first message). Nothing is sent until the person actually hits Send.

Facebook

After the user messages you, a 24-hour customer-service window starts. Inside that window you can reply freely; outside it, you may only send approved message templates (e.g., marketing/utility).

Facebook Developers
