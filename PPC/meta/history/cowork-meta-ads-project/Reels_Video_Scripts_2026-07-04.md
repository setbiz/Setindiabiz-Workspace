# Reels 9:16 Video Scripts — 4 Active Campaigns
**Date:** 4 Jul 2026 · **Why:** Opportunity Score review — no active ad set has a fullscreen vertical asset; Reels/Stories placements serve letterboxed images. Meta lift estimate: ~8% lower cost per result.
**Priority order:** 1. CCFS (scheme dies 15 Jul — ship within 48h) · 2. Virtual CFO (offer rework, June verdict "nobody converts") · 3. DPIIT · 4. Incorporation.

**Common spec:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 24–30s, audio + burned-in captions (most watch muted), hook inside first 3 seconds, keep text out of top 14% / bottom 20% (Reels UI safe zones), 2s logo end-card, UGC/selfie or simple motion-graphics style — polished corporate look underperforms on Reels.

## 1. #IND4 — CCFS (Reels flag +4, worst current CPL, HARD DEADLINE)

**Angle:** Real government deadline. 46% valid rate proves the offer works — the video just needs to carry the urgency vertically.
**Length:** 28s · **Audience:** Directors with overdue ROC filings.

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "Director — your company's pending late fees just dropped by 90%." | **90% LATE FEE WAIVER** (big, yellow highlight) | Face-to-camera or bold text slam; alarm-clock SFX |
| 3–9s | "Overdue annual returns or financial statements? Late fees keep adding up at ₹100 per day, per form." | Late fee meter counting up ₹100/day | Animated counter rising |
| 9–17s | "Under the government's CCFS 2026 scheme, file your pending forms now and pay only 10% of the late fee. The other 90% — waived." | MCA Circular 01/2026 · Pay only 10% | Stamp animation "90% WAIVED" |
| 17–23s | "But the window closes 15 July 2026. After that, full fees are back." | **DEADLINE: 15 JULY 2026** (countdown motif) | Calendar flipping to 15 Jul |
| 23–28s | "SetIndiaBiz files it end-to-end. Tap Learn More before the deadline." | Tap **Learn More** ↓ | Logo end-card + CTA arrow |

**Alt Hinglish hook (A/B):** "Company ki ROC filing pending hai? Ab late fee sirf 10% — 15 July tak."
**Compliance anchor:** CCFS-2026, MCA General Circular 01/2026 dated 24 Feb 2026; window 15 Apr–15 Jul 2026; additional fees under s.403, Companies Act 2013 read with Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014 (₹100/day for AOC-4/MGT-7). Claim only the fee waiver — no immunity/condonation-of-prosecution claims.
