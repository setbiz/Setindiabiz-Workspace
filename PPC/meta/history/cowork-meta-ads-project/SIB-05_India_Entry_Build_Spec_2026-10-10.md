## 1. Law Check (as on 10 October 2026, updated 13 October 2026)

| Point | Position | Basis | Opened |
|---|---|---|---|
| Land-border countries | China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Myanmar, Bhutan and Afghanistan stay excluded. Press Note 2 (2026 Series), issued in March 2026, lets a land-border investor hold up to 10% non-controlling beneficial ownership on the automatic route, with reporting; above that, or with any control, Government approval is still needed. The NDI (Amendment) Rules, S.O. 2174(E) of 1 May 2026, give it effect; the beneficial-ownership test looks through an intermediate entity, so a UAE or Singapore parent with land-border owners can be caught. The form no longer asks this (D10): the handler asks on the first call, before any proposal. | Press Note 3 (2020) as amended by Press Note 2 (2026); FEMA (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 | Secondary summaries (Treelife, 19 Mar 2026; Mondaq on the 1 May 2026 Rules; K S & K). Government copies of Press Note 2 and S.O. 2174(E) still not opened: dpiit.gov.in pages render but the press-note list was not reachable on 13 Oct; egazette.gov.in needs a search by S.O. number. |
| NDI Third Amendment | **S.O. 3030(E), 12 Jun 2026** (Ministry of Finance, DEA). Lets any individual resident outside India (not only NRIs and OCIs) buy and sell equity of **listed** Indian companies on a repatriation basis under Schedule III (each individual below 10%, all such individuals together up to 24%; a breach must be divested within five trading days or the whole holding is treated as FDI). Rules 12 and 13 carry a proviso: prior Government approval where the investment or transfer passes ownership or control of the listed company to entities or citizens of a country that shares a land border with India, or where the beneficial owner is such a citizen. **Not triggered by incorporating an unlisted subsidiary**, which stays under the rule 6(a) route above. Relevant only if a lead asks about portfolio investment in listed shares. | Rules 9, 12, 13 and Schedules II–III, FEMA (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019, as amended by S.O. 3030(E) | Notification text as reproduced by TaxGuru; SCC Online summary (16 Jun 2026). Gazette copy not opened. |
| NDI Fourth Amendment | **S.O. 4870(E), 2 Sep 2026**. Inserts entry 15.2.5 in Schedule I: an e-commerce entity may run an inventory-based model only for exports of goods made in India. No land-border change. Not relevant to SIB-05 ads. | Schedule I, FEMA (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 | TaxGuru reproduction (it notes no independently verified Gazette copy). Gazette copy not opened. |
| Resident director | Every company needs at least one director who stays in India for at least 182 days in the financial year. The 2020 relaxations (MCA GC 11/2020 and 36/2020) covered FY 2019-20 and 2020-21 only. | Sec. 149(3), Companies Act, 2013 | Secondary (MBG on GC 36/2020). Bare Act on indiacode.nic.in not opened. The S1 ad says only that a resident director is "arranged separately" (D21). |
| Branch and liaison office | Branch office: profit-making record in each of the preceding five financial years and net worth of at least USD 100,000. Liaison office: three years and USD 50,000. Applications in Form FNC through an AD Category-I bank; prior RBI approval for applicants from Pakistan, for some other countries opening offices in J&K, the North East or Andaman and Nicobar, and for defence, telecom, private security and broadcasting businesses. A liaison office represents the parent and promotes trade and collaboration and carries on no commercial, trading or industrial activity (Reg. 2(5), FEMA 22(R)/2016-RB); approval generally valid for three years. Offices register with the ROC once established. | RBI Master Direction No. 10/2015-16 (1 Jan 2016, last updated 18 May 2021) under FEMA 22(R)/2016-RB | rbi.org.in (government), re-read 12 Oct |
| FC-GPR | An Indian company reports an issue of equity instruments to a person resident outside India, where reckoned as FDI, in Form FC-GPR not later than 30 days from the date of issue. | Reg. 4(1), FEMA (Mode of Payment and Reporting of Non-Debt Instruments) Regulations, 2019 (FEMA 395/2019-RB), amended up to 13 Jun 2026 | rbi.org.in (government), 12 Oct |
| GST on the USD 499 fee | A service to a parent company outside India, paid in foreign exchange, is an export of services if all five conditions are met; place of supply is the recipient's location; the supply is zero-rated and billed without IGST under the LUT. GST applies if a condition fails, for example an individual client located in India or payment in rupees. | Secs. 2(6), 13(2) and 16, IGST Act, 2017 | Secondary (Razorpay, updated 3 Aug 2026) |
| EU and UK data law | Offering services to people in the EU or UK brings in the GDPR and UK GDPR: Art. 3(2)(a), the Art. 13 notice at collection, and an EU and UK representative under Art. 27 unless processing is only occasional. A campaign running for 30 days is hard to call occasional. The GDPR's "legal obligation" basis covers only EU, member-state and UK law (Art. 6(3)), so Indian record-keeping rests on legitimate interests. Disclosing data to Zoho and Meta outside the EEA is a Chapter V transfer even though Setindiabiz is in India (EDPB Guidelines 05/2021, v2.0 of 14 Feb 2023). UK: from 19 June 2026 controllers must facilitate complaints and acknowledge them within 30 days (Sec. 164A, Data Protection Act 2018, inserted by Sec. 103, Data (Use and Access) Act 2025; commenced by SI 2026/82). | Regulation (EU) 2016/679; UK GDPR; DPA 2018 | legislation.gov.uk (Sec. 164A, SI 2026/82); EDPB guidelines via secondary summaries |
| Indian data law | Consent notice on the form and a working privacy policy link. DPDP Rules, 2025 notified 13 Nov 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E)); Board provisions in force from that date; consent-manager provisions from 13 Nov 2026; notice, consent and Data Principal rights from 13 May 2027. | DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 | Secondary (AZB & Partners; Taxmann for the G.S.R. number). Gazette copy not opened. |

Never say: "100% FDI allowed", "no approval needed", "set up in X days", "no need to visit India" (not yet confirmed with operations).
