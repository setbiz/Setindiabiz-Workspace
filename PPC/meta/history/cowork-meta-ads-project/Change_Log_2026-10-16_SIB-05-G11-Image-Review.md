## Legal basis for the image fixes

**State Emblem (page 3).** State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005 (Act 50 of 2005, in force 12 Sep 2007 by S.O. 1526(E)):
- s.2(b): "emblem" is the State Emblem as described in the Schedule.
- s.3: no use of the emblem **or any colourable imitation** in a way that tends to create an impression of a connection with the Government, without Central Government permission.
- s.4: no use of the emblem for the purpose of any trade, business, calling or profession.
- s.7: contravening s.3, up to 2 years and/or fine up to ₹5,000; contravening s.4, 6 months to 2 years and fine up to ₹5,000.
- Source: indiacode.nic.in, A2005-50.pdf (opened 16 Oct; read through an excerpting fetch, so pinpoint wording should be checked against the PDF before it is quoted anywhere).

**National Flag (pages 3, 5, 6).** Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950 (Act 12 of 1950):
- s.2(a): "emblem" includes any flag specified in the Schedule.
- s.3: no use, for the purpose of any trade, business, calling or profession, of a Schedule emblem or any colourable imitation without the previous permission of the Central Government.
- Schedule item 3: the Indian National Flag. Item 16: the pictorial representation of the Ashoka Chakra as used in the National Flag.
- s.5: fine up to ₹500.
- Flag Code of India, 2002, Part II: the Flag shall not be used for commercial purposes in violation of the 1950 Act.
- Sources: indiacode.nic.in, A1950-12.pdf (opened 16 Oct); Flag Code text on government-hosted copies (barnala.punjabpolice.gov.in, eoicairo.gov.in).
- Whether a flag in the background of an ad photograph is "use for the purpose of any trade, business" is a judgement call; Sanjeev chose to remove it (D25). Both the crest and the flag also sit badly with the footer "not affiliated with any government or regulator".
