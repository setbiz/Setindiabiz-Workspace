# Connectivity by lead arrival time (2 Nov 2026)

Read-only analysis. Nothing changed in Meta or CRM. Question (Sanjeev, 2 Nov): does 24x7 delivery hurt connectivity, and what schedule should Trademark use?

**Data:** 1,803 paid Meta leads in Zoho CRM, created 7 Aug to 30 Oct 2026 (test lead excluded): 1,468 form, 335 WhatsApp. Never reached = Establishing Contact or blank, or Lost with Avoiding Call. Stage is CRM status only.

**Limit:** "Avoiding Call" is set by RMs, and the 23 Aug diagnosis found it often has no call evidence. Airtel IQ call logging reached Zoho only on 30-31 Oct 2026, so true pick-up by hour cannot be measured for this period.

Sunday is not consistent: form leads from 15 Oct show 25.0% never reached (n = 28).

## Owner effect

Never reached among non-junk leads ranges from 32% to 79% across owners with 40+ non-junk leads, and most owners are similar in office and off-hours. This is a wider spread than any time band. "Avoiding Call" habits may explain part of it.

## Trademark cost by hour (SIB-02 Copy, Meta, ₹7,408 / 87 leads)

9 AM-8 PM ₹85 per lead (43 leads), 8-11 PM ₹102 (16), 11 PM-7 AM ₹65 (24), 7-9 AM ₹137 (4).

## Counter-evidence

Deep_Analysis_2026-10-06: won revenue per lead 23:00-09:00 ₹142, against ₹199-₹459 by day and ₹256 for 20:00-23:00 (few wins, lumpy).

## Recommendation put to Sanjeev

Form ad sets 24x7 with a first-call-at-9:30 rule for overnight leads. WhatsApp ads Mon-Sat 9 AM-8 PM only. If he prefers a schedule on forms, Mon-Sat 7 AM-11 PM beats 9 AM-8 PM. Re-check with real Airtel IQ call data after 14 delivery days.
