## 3. The bigger lever: bundling, not targeting

Ad targeting can shift which ₹5,000 product you sell. **Packaging changes what a customer is worth.**

You already sell every component of the incorporation bundle separately — 137 Post-INC Key Five Docs, 53 ADT-1, 35 INC-20A, 52 GST Registrations in 90 days. Customers do buy them. They just buy them later, one at a time, after a second sales conversation each.

**Sell the first year as one package:** Incorporation + Key Five Docs + ADT-1 + INC-20A + first-year ACR. That moves a ₹4,599 median transaction toward ₹15,000+ without buying a single extra lead — and it locks in the ACR renewal that is 27% of your revenue.

Same logic on trademark: **TM Filing + Exam Report Reply as a fixed package** turns a ₹3,000 median into ₹9,000+ and removes the awkward second sale at the moment the client is anxious about an objection.

Three practical notes on measurement:

- Report **cost per won deal and first-year account value**, not cost per valid lead. On cost/valid, Trademark wins and Annual Return looks expensive. On revenue, that ranking inverts.
- Set a **different CPL ceiling per product**. A ₹500 lead is cheap for ACR at a ₹19,442 ticket and expensive for TM Filing at ₹3,000. One blended CPL target across products actively misallocates your budget.
- Meta will optimise toward whichever lead is easiest to generate. Feed CAPI a **value-weighted** conversion where you can, or the algorithm will fill your pipeline with ₹3,000 tickets.

## 4. On advertising products that are not live yet

You mentioned adding service or landing pages even where the product is not yet live. Two cautions, one practical and one legal.

**Legal.** SetIndiaBiz sells regulated services. Advertising a service you cannot currently supply risks being a *misleading advertisement* under **s.2(28) of the Consumer Protection Act 2019**, with the CCPA empowered under **s.21** to order discontinuation and impose penalties; the ASCI code applies the same test. Meta's own advertising policies prohibit misrepresenting a business's services.

**How to do it safely:** build the page, but frame it honestly as *"Register your interest — we'll notify you when this service opens"*. Do not take payment, do not state a delivery timeline, do not imply the service is currently available. That is a legitimate demand test and it is defensible.

**Practical.** Waitlist leads convert to ₹0 today. If they flow into the same campaigns and the same CRM segment, they will corrupt your cost-per-won-deal and your valid% for as long as they run. **Tag demand-test campaigns with a distinct `Lead_Source` value and exclude them from the daily scorecard** — and remember that adding any new Lead_Source value requires the CAPI rules and watchdog filters to be updated in the same change.
