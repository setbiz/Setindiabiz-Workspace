> **18. Notice for People in the EU, EEA and UK**
>
> This section applies if you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom when you visit our website, fill in one of our forms (including a lead form on Facebook or Instagram) or contact us. It gives the information required by Article 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and by Article 13 of the UK GDPR. Where this section differs from the rest of this policy, this section applies to you.
>
> **18.1 Who We Are**
>
> Setindiabiz Private Limited is the controller of your personal data. Our registered office is at Innov8 CP2, 44, Backary Portion, 2nd Floor, Regal Building, Connaught Place, New Delhi 110001, India. Write to help@setindiabiz.com, or to our Grievance Officer, Sudip Manna, at A-34, Sector 2, Noida 201301, India. We have no office in the EU, EEA or UK. The GDPR and the UK GDPR apply to us because we offer our services to people there (Article 3(2)(a)).
>
> **18.2 What We Use and Why**
>
> | Purpose | Personal data | Legal basis |
> |---|---|---|
> | Replying to your enquiry and sending a quote | Name, email address, phone number, company, job title, country, your answers to our form questions, and notes of our calls and chats with you | Steps you asked us to take before a contract, Article 6(1)(b) |
> | Delivering services you buy from us | The data described in sections 2 and 3 | Contract, Article 6(1)(b) |
> | Keeping the records Indian law requires of us, for example under the Companies Act, 2013 and the GST laws | Invoices, payment records, KYC documents and service records | Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f). The GDPR's "legal obligation" basis covers only EU, member-state and UK law (Article 6(3)), so it does not cover Indian law. |
> | Telling you about services you have not asked about | Name, email address, phone number | Consent, Article 6(1)(a) |
> | Measuring our Facebook and Instagram ads | When our team marks your enquiry as valid, or it reaches a proposal or a sale, our CRM sends Meta the stage reached, Meta's reference for your lead form, and your email address and phone number in hashed form (converted by SHA-256 into a fixed code that Meta matches against the data it already holds). For a sale, it also sends the amount. | Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) |
> | Keeping our systems secure, preventing fraud and dealing with legal claims | Technical and usage data, and records of our dealings with you | Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) |
>
> **18.3 Our Legitimate Interests**
>
> Where we rely on legitimate interests, these are our interests: replying to business enquiries and keeping a record of them; finding out which of our ads bring genuine enquiries, so we stop paying for the ones that do not; protecting our systems and clients from fraud; and keeping the records Indian law requires of an Indian company. You can ask us how we balanced these interests against your rights.
>
> **18.4 Do You Have to Give Us This Data?**
>
> We need your name, your email address or phone number, and your form answers to reply to your enquiry. Without them we cannot contact you. No law or contract requires you to give them when you make an enquiry.
>
> **18.5 Who Receives Your Data**
>
> Our team in India. Zoho, which provides our CRM, email and website chat and handles data on our instructions; our CRM is hosted in Zoho's United States data centre. Meta Platforms, which runs the lead forms on Facebook and Instagram and WhatsApp, and receives the measurement data described in 18.2; Meta also uses data under its own privacy policy (www.facebook.com/privacy/policy). If you become our client, the professionals, payment providers and authorities listed in section 5.
>
> **18.6 Transfers Outside the EU, EEA and UK**
>
> We are based in India, so we handle your data in India. Our providers handle it in the United States and other countries where they operate. India does not have an adequacy decision under Article 45 of the GDPR or under the UK GDPR. For transfers to our providers, our contracts with them include the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission in Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for UK data. You can ask for a copy of these safeguards at help@setindiabiz.com.
>
> **18.7 How Long We Keep It**
>
> We keep your enquiry for as long as we need it to reply to you and follow up, to keep a record of our dealings with you, and to deal with any claim. We review stored enquiries from time to time and delete or anonymise those we no longer need. If you become our client, section 7 applies. You can ask us to erase your enquiry at any time (18.8).
>
> **18.8 Your Rights**
>
> Articles 15 to 21 give you these rights. You can ask us for a copy of your data. You can ask us to correct it or erase it. You can ask us to restrict how we use it. Where we rely on contract or consent, you can ask us to send the data you gave us, in a machine-readable format, to you or to another organisation.
>
> You can object at any time to any use we base on legitimate interests. That includes the ad measurement in 18.2, and we will stop it for you. You can also object to direct marketing; we will then stop it.
>
> You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.
>
> Write to help@setindiabiz.com. We reply within one month. Article 12(3) lets us extend this by two months for complex or numerous requests, and we will tell you if we do.
>
> **18.9 Complaints**
>
> You can complain to us at help@setindiabiz.com, and we acknowledge every complaint within 30 days.
>
> You can also complain to a data protection authority:
> - in the EU or EEA, the authority in the country where you live, where you work or where the issue arose (Article 77), for example the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands or the data protection authority of your federal state in Germany;
> - in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
>
> You can also go to court where you live (Article 79). Section 17 does not limit this.
>
> **18.10 Automated Decisions and Profiling**
>
> We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects (Article 22). A member of our team reviews every enquiry. Meta decides who sees our ads, using its own data and the settings we choose, such as country, minimum age and interests. The measurement data in 18.2 helps Meta show our ads to people similar to our clients. Meta's privacy policy explains how it does this.
>
> **18.11 Consent**
>
> For people in the EU, EEA and UK, consent means a clear action you take for a stated purpose, such as ticking a box. Using our website or services is not consent. The consent statements in the opening paragraphs, in section 10 and in section 20 are not a legal basis for using your data.
