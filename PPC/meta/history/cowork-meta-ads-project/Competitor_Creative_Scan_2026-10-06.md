## Claims competitors use that we must NOT copy
"Lowest cost in India", "India's #1", "under MCA guidelines", "soft copy within hours", "done in 3–5 days" without qualification, "100% tax rebate" for DPIIT, "4.6 star / 100,000+" without proof (ASCI Code Ch. I.1; CPA 2019 s.2(28); CCPA Misleading Ads Guidelines 2022).

## Date check — AOC-4 / MGT-7 (AGM held 30 Sep 2026)
- s.137(1) Companies Act 2013: within 30 days of the AGM; s.92(4): within 60 days from the AGM.
- General Clauses Act 1897 s.9 excludes the first day → AOC-4 by 30 Oct, MGT-7 by 29 Nov.
- Some competitors state 29 Oct / 28 Nov (counting the AGM day). Ad copy should use "by 30 Oct" only with "(AGM on 30 Sep)", or the safer "file in October" framing — decide at claims review.
