## Law check

Read from the MCA PDF itself (general-circular-fs-ar-20261024.pdf) on 2 Nov 2026.

| Point | Position | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Relief | AOC-4 and its variants for FY 2025-26 up to 31 December 2026; MGT-7 and MGT-7A for FY 2025-26 up to 31 January 2027; without payment of additional fees | General Circular No. 05/2026, 24 October 2026, para 2 |
| AGM | No extension of time to hold AGMs; companies that missed statutory timelines stay liable under the Act | Same, para 3 |
| After the relief | All fees including additional fees, counted from the date the filing was actually due under the Act | Same, para 4 |
| Scope | FY 2025-26 only. Earlier years are not covered, so the Rs 100 a day additional fee continues for them | Same, para 2; Sec. 403, Companies Act, 2013 read with the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014 |
| LLP Form 8 | Not named in the circular. No LLP ad in this set | Same, para 2 |

## Text rules

- Do not say "due date extended". Para 3 and 4 keep the statutory position; say "without additional fee".
- Do not imply the AGM date moved.
- Quote Rs 100 a day only for earlier years, or as what applies after the relief ends.
- Code numbers continue the [ACR-Cn] pattern, which the daily watch counts.
