## **Why no exclusions appear in this document**

Meta has removed detailed-targeting exclusions. Every irrelevant cohort (students, housewives, retirees, rural, Hindi-only, feature-phone, freebie-seekers, fantasy-sports, daily-wage) is filtered out structurally by the spine: metro / Tier-2 cities + age 25–40 + English-only + role-based job titles + the "Small business owners / Page admins" narrow stack. This is the correct 2026 replacement for the old negative-interest list.

## **Apply at Ad Set level (not inside the saved audience)**

Custom-audience exclusions: CA_ALL_LEADS_180D, CA_CUSTOMERS_540D, CA_FORM_SUBMITTED_30D, CA_WHATSAPP_INITIATED_30D.

Advantage+ Audience and Detailed Targeting Expansion: confirm both are OFF on every ad set.

Versioning: never edit a master saved audience once in use — duplicate it as _V2. Editing a master shifts every live campaign using it silently.

GEO: IN14 (14 Indian cities) · 10C (10 foreign countries)

LANG: ENG · AGE: 25-40 · ROLE: dominant title/intent stack · VERSION: V1, V2…
