## 2. Law Behind the Copy (opened 12 Oct 2026)

| Point used in an ad | What the text says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FC-GPR timing (S2) | An Indian company issuing equity instruments to a person resident outside India, where the issue is reckoned as FDI, reports it in Form FC-GPR not later than thirty days from the date of issue. | Reg. 4(1), FEMA (Mode of Payment and Reporting of Non-Debt Instruments) Regulations, 2019, Notification FEMA 395/2019-RB of 17 Oct 2019, amended up to 13 Jun 2026 [rbi.org.in] |
| Branch office eligibility (E2, B2) | Profit-making track record during the immediately preceding five financial years in the home country; net worth at least USD 100,000. | RBI Master Direction No. 10/2015-16, updated as on 18 May 2021 [rbi.org.in] |
| Liaison office eligibility (E2, B1) | Profit-making track record during the immediately preceding three financial years; net worth at least USD 50,000. Approval generally valid three years. | Same Master Direction [rbi.org.in] |
| Exceptions to eligibility | An applicant that does not meet the criteria and is a subsidiary of another company may submit a Letter of Comfort from its parent. So the copy says "generally". | Same Master Direction [rbi.org.in] |
| What a liaison office cannot do (E2, B1) | A liaison office "does not undertake any commercial / trading / industrial activity, directly or indirectly" and maintains itself out of inward remittances. The regulations do not use the words "earn income", so the copy now says "cannot carry on business in India". | Reg. 2(5), FEMA 22(R)/2016-RB of 31 Mar 2016 [rbi.org.in] |
| Prior RBI approval (B2) | Needed for applicants from Pakistan; for applicants from Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan, Iran, China, Hong Kong or Macau opening in J&K, the North East or Andaman and Nicobar; and for principal business in defence, telecom, private security or information and broadcasting. | Same Master Direction [rbi.org.in] |
| Application route (B1) | Form FNC to an AD Category-I bank that the applicant chooses. The parent need not already bank there, so "your bank in India" became "an authorised bank in India". | Same Master Direction [rbi.org.in] |
| Annual filings (S2) | Annual return and financial statements filed with the Registrar every year. | Secs. 92 and 137, Companies Act, 2013 [settled] |
| Land-border investors (Canva S1 page only) | Press Note 2 (2026 Series), dated 10 Mar 2026, lets a land-border investor take up to 10% without control on the automatic route; above that, or with control, Government approval is needed. Given effect by the NDI (Amendment) Rules, S.O. 2174(E) of 1 May 2026; a Third Amendment, S.O. 3030(E) of 12 Jun 2026, adds land-border control triggers in rules 12 and 13. The approved on-image line only says such investors "follow separate rules". | [web search — verify]: K S & K, Treelife, TaxGuru. Government copies still to be opened (see section 6) |
| Resident director | Sec. 149(3), Companies Act, 2013 | Not used in any ad. indiacode.nic.in returned an error; still secondary only |

## 3. Claim-by-claim

### 🔴 Cut or change

**Claim:** "Most sectors allow foreign investment on the automatic route, subject to sectoral caps and conditions." (S1)
**Call:** 🔴 Cut. **Replaced by:** "We check your sector's foreign investment route and conditions first."
**Why:** Adds little to a price ad, needs a sector count to back "most", and edges toward the banned "no approval needed".

**Claim:** "land-border investors need prior Government approval" (Canva S1 page, small print)
**Call:** 🔴 Change. **Replaced by:** "Investors from countries sharing a land border with India follow separate rules."
**Why:** Up to 10% without control is now on the automatic route (secondary sources).

### ⚠️ Reworded (all applied in section 4)

- "A branch office needs profits in each of the last five years" / "A liaison office needs three years of profits" → "generally needs a profit-making record over the last five [three] financial years" (RBI wording; allows for the Letter of Comfort route).
- "cannot earn income in India" → "cannot carry on business in India and is funded from abroad" (Reg. 2(5), FEMA 22(R)/2016-RB).
- "We prepare the application through your bank in India." → "We prepare the application, which is filed through an authorised bank in India."
- S1 price scope: added "Registered office and a resident director are arranged separately." (decision B); "End to End" headline dropped from the price ad.
- "Government fees, stamp duty and digital signatures at actuals." → "Government fees, stamp duty and digital signature costs are extra, billed at actual cost."
- "reports its share issue to the RBI on Form FC-GPR within 30 days" → "reports each issue of shares to its foreign investor on Form FC-GPR within 30 days of the issue".
- "with one team" → "with one point of contact" (certifications may come from an outside CS or CA).
- Canva E1 typos: "FIDI route & FEMA check" → "FDI route & FEMA check"; "Fema 395/2019-RB" → "FEMA 395/2019-RB".

## 4. Approved Copy (exact text, approved 13 Oct 2026)

Never used anywhere: "100% FDI allowed", "no approval needed", "set up in X days", "no need to visit India". CTA on all ads: **Get quote**.

**Footer on every image:** Setindiabiz is a private professional services firm in India, not affiliated with any government or regulator. Government fees extra, billed at cost.

### E1, India entry (ad set A)

> Planning to set up in India? We help foreign companies choose between an Indian subsidiary, a branch office and a liaison office, then handle the filings with the Registrar of Companies and the reporting under India's foreign exchange rules (FEMA). Setindiabiz has served 25,000+ businesses from offices in Delhi, Noida, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. Answer three short questions and our India-entry team will contact you.

Headline: Set up your business in India · Description: Subsidiary, branch or liaison office

### E2, choose the structure (ad set A)

> Subsidiary, branch office or liaison office? A subsidiary is an Indian company. A branch office generally needs a profit-making record over the last five financial years and net worth of at least USD 100,000. A liaison office generally needs a three-year profit-making record and net worth of at least USD 50,000; it cannot carry on business in India and is funded from abroad. Tell us your plans and we will suggest the route that fits.

Headline: Which India structure fits you? · Description: Compare the three routes

### S1, price (ad set B)

> Set up your Indian subsidiary from USD 499. The fee covers consultation and our services up to the Certificate of Incorporation. Government fees, stamp duty and digital signature costs are extra, billed at actual cost. Registered office and a resident director are arranged separately. We check your sector's foreign investment route and conditions first.

Headline: Indian subsidiary from USD 499 · Description: Government fees extra, at cost

### S2, after incorporation (ad set B)

> Incorporation is the first step. A foreign-owned Indian company reports each issue of shares to its foreign investor on Form FC-GPR within 30 days of the issue, then files its accounts and annual return with the Registrar of Companies every year. Setindiabiz handles incorporation and the compliance that follows, with one point of contact.

Headline: Your India compliance, one contact · Description: Incorporation, FEMA and annual filings

### B1, liaison office (ad set C)

> Want a presence in India before you trade? A liaison office can represent your company and promote trade and technical or financial collaboration with Indian businesses. It cannot carry on business in India and is funded from abroad. Eligibility: generally a profit-making record over the last three financial years and net worth of at least USD 50,000. We prepare the application, which is filed through an authorised bank in India.

Headline: Open a liaison office in India · Description: 3 years' profits, USD 50,000 net worth

### B2, branch office (ad set C)

> A branch office lets your company carry out the activities the RBI permits for branches in India. Eligibility: generally a profit-making record over the last five financial years and net worth of at least USD 100,000. Applicants in some sectors and from some countries need prior RBI approval. We check eligibility and prepare the application.

Headline: Open a branch office in India · Description: 5 years' profits, USD 100,000 net worth
