## Compliance flags
- "100% compliance" and "Affordable, fast, and reliable": guarantee and comparative-type claims. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 s.2(28); CCPA Misleading Ads Guidelines 2022; ASCI Code Ch. I. Substantiate or remove.
- "Software Engineers, Register Now\!" and "Are you a software engineer…": addressing a profession isn't one of Meta's listed personal-attribute categories, but check the current Meta Personal Attributes policy before launch.
- The "Register Now"/"Register Your Company Today\!" CTA must not imply Setindiabiz is the registering authority (MCA). Keep wording such as "We help you register".
