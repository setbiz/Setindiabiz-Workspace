## File-level status and compliance (legal refresh needed before use)
- **Status:** Flow v3 (turn 5) is the latest and is **CURRENT as a structural draft**. Flow v1 (turn 3) and flow v2 (turn 4) are **SUPERSEDED** by v3. v2's name-and-email-first order was carried into v3, and v2's card sequence and data-capture table are still useful. Several benefit claims are probably **OUTDATED**; see below.
- **Angel tax:** cards and flows say "Angel Tax Exemption (56 (2)(viib))". The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 made s.56(2)(viib) of the Income-tax Act, 1961 inapplicable from AY 2025-26 (verify). If so, the claim was already outdated on the chat date and must be removed.
- **80-IAC:** the deduction needs Inter-Ministerial Board certification, not DPIIT recognition alone, and covers 3 consecutive years out of the first 10. Check the incorporation cut-off date and the section number under the Income-tax Act, 2025, in force from 1 Apr 2026 (verify). "Tax-Free Profits for 3 Years" and "Save 100% tax" overstate the benefit (CPA 2019 s.2(28); CCPA Guidelines 2022 Guideline 4).
- **IP rebates:** "up to 80% rebate on government fees" / "80% rebate on Patent/Trademark fees". The 80% reduction applies to patent fees (Patents Rules, 2003, startup category). Trademark startup fees are a different (lower) concession under the Trade Marks Rules, 2017 (verify both current fee schedules). "Fast-track" examination: expedited patent examination for startups is under Patents Rules r.24C; trademark expedited examination is open to any applicant on payment of a fee.
- **Labour self-certification:** the cards say "9 labour & environment laws", but the flows say "9 labour laws", which is inconsistent. Startup India lists 6 labour + 3 environmental laws, and the four Labour Codes (reported in force from 21 Nov 2025; verify) replaced most of the labour statutes.
- **Government tenders:** "without prior experience or turnover conditions" and "Recognition ensures priority access". The relaxation is subject to GFR 2017 / Department of Expenditure orders and to quality and technical specifications. "Priority access" is unsupported, so remove it.
- **Startup definition / eligibility:** check DPIIT notification G.S.R. 127(E) of 19 Feb 2019 and its amendments (verify). The card says "< 10 Years Old" while the text says "up to 10 years old".
- **SISFS:** "₹20 lakh – ₹50 lakh" and "Not received funding above ₹10 lakh from other sources". The scheme's condition concerns monetary support under other Central/State Government schemes, not funding from any source, and it has an age limit at application. Check whether the scheme is still open (verify).
- **Government association (Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950; Startup India branding):** "an official Government of India Startup Badge", "India's Official Startup Badge" and "a government recognition". These describe DPIIT, but must never suggest that Setindiabiz is official or affiliated. Do not use the Startup India or DPIIT logos.
- **Superlatives and promises (ASCI Code Ch. I; CCPA Guidelines 2022):** "India's leading Startup Legal Partner", "India's leading Startup Consulting Company", "Apply today, get recognised this week!", "2-5 working days", "register your startup within 3–5 days", "support till approval" and "100% online". Remove the superlatives and qualify every timeline.
- **Data capture (DPDP Act 2023 s.5 notice, s.6 consent; DPDP Rules 2025, phased):** name and email are collected in the first two nodes with no notice. Add a short notice and consent line before the name question. The "Auto-remind if user drops off" idea and the email follow-ups need WhatsApp Business opt-in and template rules (verify the current Meta WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy). Calls need TRAI TCCCPR 2018 compliance.
- **URLs:** the bot uses `/startup-india-recognition`, `/startup-80iac-tax-exemption` and `/startup-india-seed-fund`, but business-context lists `/startup-india-dpiit-recognition` and `/dpiit-startup-tax-exemption-80iac`. Check every link before use.
