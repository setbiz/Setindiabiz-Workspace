# SIB-04 replacement copy: MCA General Circular 05/2026 (DRAFT, needs owner approval)

**Why:** the live ACR ads say MGT-7 is due 29 Nov and ₹100/day applies after that. Per MCA General Circular No. 05/2026 dated 24 Oct 2026, for FY 2025-26 **no additional fee** is charged if AOC-4 (incl. variants) is filed by **31 Dec 2026** and MGT-7/7A by **31 Jan 2027**. The circular **does not** extend AGM timelines (s. 96) and does not cover other forms. If a company files after these windows, the additional fee is reported to run from the original due dates (s. 137(1), s. 92(4), s. 403 + Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014).
Sources: secondary (TaxGuru, CAclubindia, Kanakkupillai, corplawupdates). **Verify the circular PDF on mca.gov.in before publishing.**
