## Compliance review (read before reusing anything below)

| Item in copy | Problem | Rule | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Starting at ₹1,999" / "From ₹1,999" / "Starting ₹1,999" | ChatGPT made up this price. The site publishes no package prices (`shared/business-context.md`), so the ad would not match its landing page. | CPA 2019 s.2(28); CCPA Misleading Ads Guidelines 2022 (bait ads); ASCI Code Ch. I | Remove it, or use it only after a matching price is live on the landing page. |
| "New companies must file critical forms within 30 days of incorporation" | Wrong. INC-20A is due within 180 days (Companies Act 2013 s.10A). Only the first-auditor deadline is 30 days (s.139(6)). The carousel copy in turn 10 says 180 days correctly, so the copy contradicts itself. | CPA 2019 s.2(28) | State each deadline correctly, with its section. |
| "Avoid penalties up to ₹1 lakh" / "₹50,000 or more" | Under s.10A(2) the company pays ₹50,000. Each officer in default pays ₹1,000/day, up to ₹1,00,000. "Up to ₹1 lakh" as a company penalty, and "₹50,000 **or more**", both overstate it. | CPA 2019 s.2(28); ASCI Ch. I | Quote it as: "company ₹50,000; officers ₹1,000/day up to ₹1 lakh (s.10A(2), Companies Act 2013 — verify current text)". |
| "Swipe Up to Get Compliant" | **OUTDATED** wording. Instagram replaced Swipe Up with the link sticker in 2021, and Stories ads use a CTA button. | Platform | Use the CTA button label. |
| "Use minimal text (under 20% rule)" (image size table) | **OUTDATED.** Meta dropped the 20% text rule in 2020–21. Less text is still a good practice, but it is no longer a rule. | Platform | Treat it as guidance only. |
| Uploading the MCA incorporation list (directors' email/mobile) as a Custom Audience, and building a Lookalike from it | This is personal data the directors never gave to Setindiabiz. Meta's Custom Audience terms require you to have the right to upload it. | DPDP Act 2023 ss.4–6, s.8 (s.3(c)(ii) "publicly available" exemption needs legal review); DPDP Rules 2025 phased to May 2027 (verify on meity.gov.in); Meta Custom Audience Terms | Get legal sign-off before uploading. Prefer consented lists. Never commit the list to this repo. |
| "Dematerialisation Compliance (if applicable)" | Applies to private companies other than small companies under PAS Rules r.9B (2023). The deadline has been extended; verify the current one. | Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules 2014, r.9B | Keep "if applicable" and link to an explainer. |
