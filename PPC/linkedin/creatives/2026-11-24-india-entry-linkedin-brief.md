## Rules (same as the 13 Oct brief)
- The footer must be readable on a phone. At 1200 px wide, use about 26 px or more.
- No client logos, no timelines ("in X days"), no "100% FDI", no "no approval needed", no "no need to visit India".
- No government emblem, MCA or RBI logo, and no words like lawyer, attorney or law firm.
- India Gate and city skyline photos are fine (already approved).
- Keep the main text inside the middle 90% of the image. LinkedIn crops the edges in some feeds.
- Any new wording goes back to Sanjeev before use.
