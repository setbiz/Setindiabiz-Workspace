## Compliance notes
- "Our expert team at Setindiabiz helps you avoid penalties and ensures full ROC compliance": "ensures full … compliance" is an absolute claim that needs substantiation. CPA 2019 s.2(28); ASCI Code Ch. I.
- The Matched Audience is built from the MCA director list. DPDP Act 2023 ss.4–6 and s.8 apply, with legal review of the s.3(c)(ii) exemption (see `chatgpt-07-matched-audience-pilot.md`).
- Message Ads cannot be delivered to members in the EU (LinkedIn policy since 2022; verify). This is not relevant for India-only targeting.
