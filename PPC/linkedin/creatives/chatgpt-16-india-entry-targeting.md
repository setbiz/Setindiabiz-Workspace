## Compliance and platform notes
- **Targeting EU/UK/US individuals:** the GDPR, UK GDPR/PECR and CAN-SPAM may apply to follow-up email outreach. Turn 16 suggests "Purchase or build lists of: Export companies, Manufacturers, SaaS firms, Companies hiring in Asia". Purchased lists for cold email to the EU/UK carry high regulatory risk. Get legal review.
- **Message/Conversation Ads** are not deliverable to EU members (LinkedIn policy, verify). Use Sponsored Content plus Lead Gen Forms for Germany, the Netherlands, France, Ireland and Sweden.
- **"Trusted by global companies entering India."** This needs substantiation (client list or logos with permission). CPA 2019 s.2(28); ASCI Code Ch. I. The site shows "Trusted By Leading Brands" and client logos (turn 18 audit).
- **"Launch your India subsidiary in weeks, not months."** This timeline claim must match real turnaround data (DSC → COI; apostille is often the bottleneck).
- Add the private-firm disclaimer to every landing page (verbatim, turn 16): "Setindiabiz is a private legal, tax and compliance consulting firm. We are not affiliated with the Government of India or the Ministry of Corporate Affairs."

## Verbatim assets

### Qualified lead definition (turn 16)
> A lead should satisfy at least one of these:
> Foreign company expanding to India
> Foreign startup establishing Indian operations
> Foreign investor setting up a wholly owned subsidiary
> Overseas company opening Branch Office/Liaison Office/Project Office
> Indian-origin entrepreneur living abroad incorporating in India
> VC-backed startup establishing an India GCC
> Manufacturing company entering India
> SaaS company hiring employees in India
>
> Do not optimize for: Students; Visa seekers; Job seekers; People searching "company registration" for local Indian businesses; Freelancers

### Country tiers (turn 16)
> Tier 1 Countries — Highest priority: USA, UK, Canada, Singapore, UAE, Australia, Germany, Netherlands. Highest CPC but highest quality.
> Tier 2: France, Ireland, Japan, South Korea, Israel, Switzerland, Sweden
> Tier 3: Malaysia, Hong Kong, New Zealand, Saudi Arabia, Qatar

### Buyer personas (turn 16)
> Persona 1 — CEO of SME — Company Size 10–200 employees — Thinking "We now need India."
> Persona 2 — General Counsel — Searching: Legal compliance, FDI, Subsidiary
> Persona 3 — Finance Director — Looking for: Tax, Accounting, GST, Payroll
> Persona 4 — International Expansion Manager — Keywords: India Market Entry, India Expansion, India Subsidiary
> Persona 5 — VC-backed Startup Founder — Needs: Indian development team, Sales office, Hiring
