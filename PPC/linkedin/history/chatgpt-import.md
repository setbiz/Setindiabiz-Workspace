## #10 LinkedIn vs Twitter PPC (LinkedIn parts)
- **Stated date:** Jan 29, 2025 at 7:59 AM
- **URL:** https://chatgpt.com/c/6a530e20-b710-832d-bd90-f53c036ed8bd
- **Summary:** Sanjeev wanted PPC "out of google ads and Microsoft" and asked about Twitter or LinkedIn. ChatGPT favoured LinkedIn. Sanjeev then asked for other options (ChatGPT: Facebook, Reddit "7 out of 10", Quora, niche platforms), raised his difficulty with Facebook audience setting, and asked the general cost of LinkedIn ads. Last, he asked which platform best reaches small businesses for accounting, noting "I think they are not on inkedin as these guys are small enterpreneurs". ChatGPT pointed to Facebook/Instagram, Google Search and local directories or forums. No platform was chosen.
- **Decisions and reasons:** none final. Sanjeev's own view (turn 6): small business owners are not on LinkedIn, because "these guys are small enterpreneurs".
- **Account changes:** none.
- **Metrics (ChatGPT's general benchmarks, no source shown, as of 29 Jan 2025, USD; not Setindiabiz data):**
  - CPC: "$5–$10 per click is a common benchmark, though it can go higher in competitive industries or for highly specific targeting."
  - CPM: "$6–$12 is a common range for impressions-based campaigns"
  - Lead Gen Form CPL: "the cost per lead typically ranges from $30–$100 depending on the industry, campaign optimization, and the quality of the targeting."
- **Verbatim: LinkedIn rationale (turn 1, ChatGPT):** "Between Twitter and LinkedIn, LinkedIn might be the better platform for Setindiabiz. Since your services primarily target businesses, startups, and professionals—especially in areas like compliance, legal, and accounting—LinkedIn's audience aligns more closely with your goals. On LinkedIn, you can target decision-makers, entrepreneurs, and small business owners who would naturally be interested in the kind of guidance and services you provide."
- **Verbatim: why LinkedIn costs more, and saving tips (turn 5, ChatGPT):** "LinkedIn is more expensive because it's a professional network. It allows you to target users by their job title, company size, industry, and seniority level, which can yield higher-quality leads. Many advertisers find that even though the CPC is higher, the resulting conversions or leads are more valuable because they're reaching the exact audience they need." Cost-saving tips: "Start with a lower daily budget and optimize based on performance." / "Test multiple ad formats (Sponsored Content, Message Ads, Text Ads) to find the most cost-effective option." / "Refine your targeting over time to focus on the segments that convert best, reducing wasteful clicks."
- **Verbatim: small-business answer (turn 6, ChatGPT, summary line):** "In summary, Facebook/Instagram and Google Ads are often more effective for reaching smaller entrepreneurs and local business owners who might not have a strong presence on LinkedIn."
- **Worked / didn't:** nothing was tested. The CRM gives indirect support to the turn 6 view: Meta (Facebook + Instagram) produced about 96% of ad-attributed won ₹ (corrected) in Nov 2025–Oct 2026. But Google Ads produced 84 leads and 0 won, which contradicts the "Google Search Ads" half of that answer (possibly a tracking or tagging gap; see business-context observations).
- **Open items (from the chat):** "testing LinkedIn ad formats if LinkedIn is used". No platform was chosen.

## #15 Advertising on ChatGPT and Perplexity (LinkedIn cross-reference)
- **Stated date:** Aug 30, 2025. **URL:** https://chatgpt.com/c/fbeee4d7-fa60-8326-98cb-5378721b9a50
- **LinkedIn items (ChatGPT's US plan, verbatim, never executed):**
  - Week 4–6: "LinkedIn with Lead Gen Forms and Document Ads (gate the PDF). Forms prefill profile data (higher CVR)."
  - Budget: "LinkedIn (Lead Gen + Document Ads): $50/day" (with Perplexity) or "LinkedIn $60/day" (without), within USD 200/day.
  - "Formats: Lead Gen Forms (prefilled), Document Ads (gate your PDFs), Website Conversions."
  - **Targeting:** "Job functions Legal, Operations, Founders; skills startup, venture capital, import/export, trademarks; Company HQ in target metros."
  - **Lead Form fields:** "(keep 3–4): Name, Work Email, Company, Country. (3–4 fields recommended.)"
  - **Offer hooks:** "Free US→India Incorporation Kit (PDF)", "ODI & APR 2025 Checklist."
  - **Document Ad:** "Headline: US→India Incorporation Kit (2025) / Intro: Everything a US founder needs to set up an Indian Pvt Ltd—steps, docs, fees, FEMA notes. / Gate with Lead Gen Form (Name, Work Email, Company)."
  - Tracking: "LinkedIn Insight Tag". Week 7–12: "Start LinkedIn newsletter from the blog stream."
  - Expectation: "Shift budget toward channels producing SQOs (expect LinkedIn and Microsoft Search to lead; Reddit/X assist)."
  - Compliance: "LinkedIn Lead Gen data use".
- Full verbatim: `chatgpt-ads/creatives/chatgpt-15-us-gtm-multichannel.md`.
- **Status:** **OUTDATED** (Aug 2025; "2025" in the offer names). This is the most concrete LinkedIn plan in the import and is a reasonable template for a foreign-founder / India-entry test. "Founders" is not a LinkedIn job function, so check the current targeting facets. "Free … Kit" must be genuinely free (CCPA Misleading Ads Guidelines 2022, free-claims provision; verify the clause).
- **DPDP note:** LinkedIn Lead Gen Form data that flows into Zoho CRM is personal data. The form needs a privacy-policy link and a purpose statement (DPDP Act 2023 s.5 notice, s.6 consent). Do not reuse it for Matched Audiences beyond the stated purpose. The DPDP Rules 2025 commence in phases through about May 2027, so verify the current status on meity.gov.in.
