# Ad Copy Standard

## Passes when
- Each ad states the service, the outcome, and a clear next step.
- Price claims are complete ("₹X + govt fees" where applicable). "Free" and "lowest" are substantiated or removed.
- There is no implied government affiliation. Every superlative or time claim ("in 7 days") has evidence on file.
- Headline and description character limits are met for the platform.
- The ad message matches the landing page (`PPC/shared/landing-pages.md`).
- The copy follows `PPC/shared/brand-voice.md`.
- Each ad scores at least 4/5 for clarity and trust from `ppc-customer-lens`.

## Blockers
- Any finding marked Blocker by `ppc-compliance-reviewer`.
