# Campaign Plan Standard (PPC adaptation of the EGM Heilmeier standard)

A campaign or test plan passes if a competent media buyer **who has never seen this account** can launch it without asking a single question, and the CFO can see the maximum downside.

## Required sections. All must be answered explicitly.
1. **Objective (no jargon)**: what business result, for which service, in which geography.
2. **Current state**: how this service is acquired today, with numbers and their source and date range. If there's no data, say so.
3. **Approach and why it should work**: platform, campaign type, targeting or keywords, bid strategy, creative angle. Cite learnings or data.
4. **Unit economics**: AOV, lead→sale %, break-even CPL and CPA, target CPL and CPA.
5. **Budget and cap**: daily and total test budget, maximum loss, duration (at least long enough to exit learning).
6. **Measurement**: primary metric, conversion event, tracking tag status, CRM reconciliation method, decision threshold.
7. **Kill / scale rules**: pre-registered, numeric, with dates.
8. **Risks**: policy, compliance (cite provisions), cannibalisation, lead quality, team capacity.
9. **Build sheet**: exact campaign, ad group or ad set names (naming convention), settings, ads (verbatim copy), extensions or assets, audiences, negatives, URLs with UTMs. Created **PAUSED**.
10. **Review date**: when the result is evaluated, with the entry to add to `changelog.md`.

## Failing conditions (Blockers)
- Any metric without a source, or any estimate not labelled as one.
- No cap or kill rule.
- Ad copy not screened by `ppc-compliance-reviewer`.
- A build sheet that leaves a decision to whoever implements it.
