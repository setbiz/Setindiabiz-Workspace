| ID | Severity | Section | Issue | Evidence | Fix |

| PC-1 | Blocker | §4 Unit economics, §7 TM rules | The Trademark AOV most likely includes the government fee, so break-even is overstated about 3x. The "Trademark breaks even at organic AOV" reading, the ₹3,000 kill line and the ₹1,500–3,000 "continue" band all rest on it. | §8: fee ₹1,999 + GST + govt fee ₹4,500/class. ₹1,999 + ₹4,500 = ₹6,499, close to the organic AOV of ₹5,953 and the all-source AOV of ₹6,568 (§2). §2 caveat: "Amount may include government fees (unconfirmed)". At 50% margin on ₹1,999, break-even CPA is about ₹1,000, not ₹2,977. | Before launch, split one sample of won TM deals into professional fee and govt fee in Zoho. Recompute break-even on the professional fee only. Re-set the TM kill line to ≤ break-even (e.g. kill > ₹1,000–1,200) and remove the "continue" band above break-even. |
| PC-2 | Major | §4, §7 AF rules | The same AOV problem affects Annual Filing. The advertised packages are ₹4,599, ₹5,999 and ₹7,999 + GST & ROC fee, but the AOV used is ₹12,780–14,647. That is 2–3x the package price, and the plan itself says those figures include bundles. The ≤ ₹4,000 target CPA is set against an AOV the ads don't sell. | §2 caveat (bundles); §8 price row; §4 table. | Use won AF deals whose Amount sits in the package range (or the professional-fee field) as the AOV. Re-derive break-even and the target CPA, and state them as [calc] with the subset used. |
| PC-3 | Major | §7 Kill rules (both) | The kill gates are far looser than the plan's own targets and break-even figures, so they allow sustained loss. | TM day 7: <10 leads at ₹10,000 means a CPL of up to ₹1,000 still passes, against a target of ₹150 and a §4 break-even of ₹35–238. TM day 14 kills only above ₹300 CPL (2x target). AF day 7: <8 leads at ₹8,000 lets ₹1,000 CPL pass. AF day 14 kills above ₹800, which is above break-even even at 8% lead→won (₹511–586). AF has no cost-per-won checkpoint until 31 Jan, by which time ≈₹99k is spent. | Tie the gates to the targets: day 7 pause if CPL > 2x target (TM ₹300, AF ₹800), day 14 pause if CPL > break-even CPL at 8%. Add an AF won-deal checkpoint on 31 Dec (pause if 0 won after ≥₹40k). Note the deal-close lag and measure won deals by lead cohort. |
| PC-4 | Major | §6 Measurement (Tags) | The plan says Zoho classifies `utm_source=google` + `cpc` as `Advt - Google`. The runbook doesn't support that. Without a GCLID, a paid lead with a google.com referrer would fall to rule 5 and be labelled `SEO - Google`. That breaks the primary metric and hides cannibalisation (PC-8). | `lead-source-attribution-runbook.md` §5: rule 1 uses only click IDs (gclid/gbraid/wbraid/gad_source) for Advt - Google. The `utm_medium=cpc` branch (line 277) applies only to fb/ig/meta sources. Audit: only 6 of 84 Google leads had a gclid in Source_Url. | Add a classifier rule that maps utm_source=google + utm_medium=cpc to Advt - Google, placed before rule 5. Make G4 explicit: the test lead must arrive without a gclid and still land as Advt - Google. Answer D4 as "wait" until both are true. |
| PC-5 | Major | §3, §9 Keywords | The "Due Date Searches" AF ad group, and arguably "TM Fees and Cost", target informational/DIY intent. This repeats the Dec-25 mistake ("trademark search" took 79% of spend). Under Maximize Clicks, cheap informational queries win the budget. | Handoff volumes: "aoc 4 due date" 3,600 and "due date for roc filing" 1,900 make up about 57% of the listed AF volume (≈9,580/month). Launch AF without Due Date Searches, or run it at a lower CPC cap (e.g. ₹30) behind a day-3 search-terms review. Apply the same treatment to TM fee queries ("government fee", "fees list", "form TM-A fee" as negatives). |
| PC-6 | Major | §9 Build sheet ("no shared lists"), §3 | The plan deliberately leaves the shared negative list out. The plan also tolerates up to 40% junk spend at day 7. | Audit §5.3: "exact/phrase only, plus an attached shared negative list". Review search terms daily for days 1–3. Cut the junk tolerance to ≤15%. |
| PC-7 | Blocker | §9 Build sheet | The build sheet doesn't meet the standard. There is no verbatim ad copy, keyword list, negative list, assets list or final URLs. | PC-8 | Major | §8 Cannibalisation | Organic Google already wins 852 deals a year for these two services (532 AF, 320 TM), and paid will bid on the same generic terms.
