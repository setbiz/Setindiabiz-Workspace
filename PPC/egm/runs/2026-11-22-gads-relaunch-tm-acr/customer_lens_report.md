## Findings

### CL-1 · Major · All TM and AF ads (trust)
**What I felt:** "Private Professional Firm" tells me what you're *not*. It doesn't tell me why to pick you over my CA friend or a ₹999 site. There are no reviews, no years in business, no proof you exist. The old "18,000+" claim was rightly removed, but nothing replaced it.
**Fix:** add one proof headline backed by evidence on file. "4 offices pan-India" is already substantiated (`business-context.md`), so use **"Offices in 4 Indian Cities"** (26) now. Once the screenshot and date are on file, add **"4.8★ Rated on Google"** (20) or Google seller ratings. Demote "Private Professional Firm" to a callout only, since the pinned disclaimer already covers non-affiliation.

### CL-2 · Major · All Annual Filing ads + TM "Our Fee ₹1,999 + GST" (incomplete price in some RSA combinations)
**What I felt:** "Packs From ₹4,599 + GST", "Essential Pack ₹4,599", "Complete Pack ₹7,999", "ADT-1 Filing ₹1,500" and "Our Fee ₹1,999 + GST" look like the full price. The govt or ROC fee appears only in *some* descriptions. With the disclaimer pinned in one slot, combinations such as "Complete Pack ₹7,999" + "Accounting and statutory audit are not included…" + disclaimer show no ROC fee at all. That's drip pricing (CPA 2019 s. 2(28); CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023, drip pricing).
**Fix:** put the extra fee inside every price headline: **"Packs ₹4,599 + GST + ROC Fee"** (28), **"1 Form ₹2,999 + GST + ROC Fee"** (29), **"₹1,999 + GST + Govt Fee"** (23, replaces "Our Fee ₹1,999 + GST"). Drop "Essential Pack ₹4,599" and "Complete Pack ₹7,999" as standalone headlines, or pin a description that always carries "GST & ROC fee extra".

### CL-3 · Major · TM Registration, Brand and Logo, TM Fees and Cost (total cost and the ₹4,500 vs ₹9,000 tier)
**What I felt:** I have to add ₹1,999 + 18% GST + ₹4,500 myself, and I'm not sure I'm a "small firm". If I'm a new Pvt Ltd, do I pay ₹9,000? I could end up with a surprise bill.
**Fix:** name the eligible categories and give one worked total. Description: **"₹4,500/class for individuals, DPIIT startups & MSMEs. Others ₹9,000/class. GST on our fee."** (90). Alternative: **"1 class, individual: ₹1,999 + ₹360 GST + ₹4,500 govt fee = ₹6,859 total."** (72). Provision: Trade Marks Rules 2017, First Schedule (fee entry for TM-A; the reduced fee applies to an individual, startup or small enterprise as defined in r. 2). Re-check the latest Schedule on ipindia.gov.in or India Code before use.

### CL-4 · Major · TM Registration, Brand and Logo (pinned disclaimer can be the only description shown)
**What I felt:** on mobile Google often shows one description. If it's only "Setindiabiz is a private professional services firm, not affiliated with IP India.", I see a legal note and no offer, and I scroll past.
**Fix:** merge the disclaimer with the offer in pinned description 1: **"Private firm, not affiliated with IP India. Our fee ₹1,999 + GST + govt fee."** (76). Do the same on Annual Filing if its disclaimer is pinned.

### CL-5 · Major · Brand and Logo Registration
**What I felt:** "Protect Your Logo & Slogan" + "Brand Name Registration" + one price makes me think ₹1,999 covers my name *and* my logo. If they're separate applications, I'll feel cheated on the call.
**Fix:** headline **"Name & Logo Filed Separately"** (28), or description **"Name and logo are separate applications. Our fee and govt fee apply to each one."** (80). If a combined filing is offered, say so instead.

### CL-6 · Major · Annual Compliance, ROC Annual Filing ad A (audit not included = hidden cost)
**What I felt:** "Accounting and statutory audit are not included. Clear scope, no surprise charges." But AOC-4 needs audited accounts, so I *will* pay someone else. The "no surprise" line now feels like a surprise. I also don't know what the "Extended ₹5,999" pack adds.
**Fix:** description **"Accounts must be audited first. Audit is not in the pack; ask us for a written quote."** (85). In the pack line, say in a few words what Extended adds over Essential, or drop it from the ad. AOC-4 must attach audited financial statements: Companies Act 2013 s. 137 read with s. 129 and s. 143.

### CL-7 · Major · Due Date Searches, ROC Annual Filing ad B (unexplained deadline and "original due date")
**What I felt:** "No late fee till 31 Dec" sounds too good, or like a pressure tactic. Who granted it? Then "₹100 per day… from the original due date": what *is* the original date? I'm scared but no wiser.
**Fix:** headline **"Late-Fee Waiver Till 31 Dec"** (27), with the LP naming the MCA circular (GC 05/2026, dated 24 Oct 2026 per the plan; verify the PDF on mca.gov.in before enabling). Description **"Miss it and ₹100/day per form runs from the normal Oct/Nov due date. File now."** (78). Provision: Companies Act 2013 s. 403 + Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules 2014. Pause or edit on 31 Dec as §7 of the plan says.

### CL-8 · Minor · AOC-4 and MGT-7 Forms, ROC Annual Filing (jargon and "at actuals")
**What I felt:** "Protect Your Directors' DIN", "AOC-4 Certification Included" and "ROC fee… at actuals" mean nothing to me. "At actuals" sounds open-ended.
**Fix:** **"Keep Directors Qualified"** (24) in place of the DIN headline. Description **"ROC fee ₹200-₹600 per form, based on share capital. GST extra on our fee."** (73). Source: Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules 2014, Annexure, fee table by nominal share capital; verify the current slabs on mca.gov.in. Keep "AOC-4 Certification Included" only in the forms group, where the searcher knows the term.

### CL-9 · Minor · All TM ads ("Filed in 24 Hrs of Documents")
**What I felt:** the grammar is off, and I half-read it as "registered in 24 hours". When I later learn registration takes months, I'll distrust everything else.
**Fix:** **"Filed Within 24 Hrs of Docs"** (27) plus **"Get Your TM Application No."** (27), or add to the validity description that registration itself takes months. Keep the LP evidence for the 24 h claim on file (ASCI Code Ch. I).

### CL-10 · Major · All ads (landing-page match and next step)
**What I felt:** I can't check that the page shows ₹1,999 / ₹4,599. `shared/landing-pages.md` is empty and `business-context.md` (2 Nov) says "no package prices are published". If I land on strike-through "discount" prices (W4 still open), I bounce. "Talk to a CA/CS Expert Today" with no reply time feels like a sales trap, and an 18:45 lead won't get a call "today" (office 9:30–18:30, ad schedule 09:00–19:00).
**Fix:** record both LPs with their prices in `landing-pages.md` and update `business-context.md` pricing before G1. Remove strike-through prices before launch. Align the ad schedule to 09:30–18:30 or use **"Talk to a CA/CS Expert"** (22). Once G3 is named and the SLA is met, add **"Call Back Within 2 Work Hrs"** (27).

(TM Search Test, paused: "Expert Search Report" doesn't say whether it's included in ₹1,999. Use **"Trademark Search Included"** (25) if true, before it is ever enabled.)
